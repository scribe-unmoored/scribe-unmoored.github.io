The Atronach


Born in the ashlands, beneath the stars of the Atronach, to parents whose names he no longer remembers.

As a boy, he came to the city isle, where he was raised in the shadow of White-Gold Tower. When he came of age, his natural talents for Destruction magic earned him a career as a Legion battlemage. The Atronach sign granted him spell absorption, a rare and powerful ability, while his dark elven blood could preserve him against the very fire he summoned.

This unique combination of natural abilities brought him under the scrutiny of the Blades, the Emperor’s personal guard and elite intelligence network. Steve joined their ranks in 3E389, as a witchhunter.
